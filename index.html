Balatro OS

Introduction

Balatro is the favorite game of many people, made by Indie developer LocalThunk, and nominated for game of the year in 2024. Balatro is a rougelike deckbuilder game where you collect jokers in order to create a unique poker experience. Additionally, the game also features tarot and spectral cards in oder to enhance cards in your deck in many different ways, such as adding a modifier to a card. The game's base poker features include all common poker hands found in most poker games, like Texas Hold 'Em, but the game also contains unique poker hands only possible due to spectral and tarot cards, such as five of a kind or flush house.

Balatro fun Fact: all of Balatro's music was fully created and developed by a composer on Fiverr by the name of LouisF.
